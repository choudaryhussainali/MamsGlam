Good to know

Questions, answered

How long until my order ships?

Everything is cast after you order. Please allow 3–5 working days of making and curing, then shipping time on top — tracked, worldwide.

What if a piece arrives damaged?

Tell us within 30 days with a photo, and we will remake and reship it — no forms, no fuss. Concrete is tougher than it looks, but parcels occasionally disagree.

Can I order a custom colour?

Often, yes — custom pours start from six pieces. Start a message with “Custom” and tell us the colour you dream in.

Do you do wholesale?

We love kindred stockists. Start a message with “Trade” and we will send the line sheet and lead times.
