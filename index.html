The maker

Poured slowly,
in small batches

Mams Glam began at a kitchen table, with one shell-shaped mould and a wish to make something soft for a busy world. Today every tray and dish is still poured by hand, one colour at a time, then left to cure for a full week before it is sanded, sealed and sent to you.

“No two pieces are ever quite the same — that is not a flaw. That is the signature.”

Mams GlamFounder & maker

Made by hand

Four slow steps, one calm week

  1. 01

    Design

    Every piece begins as a sketch and a colour study in our palette of chalky, calming tones.

  2. 02

    Pour

    The mineral mix is poured by hand into moulds — one piece at a time, never rushed.

  3. 03

    Cure

    A full week on the shelf gives each piece its cool, stone-like weight.

  4. 04

    Finish

    Sanded silky, sealed, wrapped in tissue and tied — ready for its new home.
